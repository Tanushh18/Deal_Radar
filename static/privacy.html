Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

DealRadar ("we", "our", "the app") helps you discover deals. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

No account required

You don't need to sign up or log in to use DealRadar. We don't collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier.

What we do collect

  • A random device ID — generated on your device and stored locally, used only to remember your saved deals, price alerts, and followed brands/stores. It isn't linked to your identity.
  • A push notification token — only if you enable notifications, so we can send you price-drop alerts and updates you've opted into. You can turn this off anytime in your device settings.

We don't use analytics SDKs, ad trackers, or any tool that profiles you across apps or sites.

Third-party links

Deals link out to third-party stores (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and similar). Once you tap through, you're on their site and subject to their own privacy policy — we don't control what they collect.

Local storage

Your saved deals, alerts, and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes this data completely — there's no server-side account to delete because none exists.

Children's privacy

DealRadar isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
